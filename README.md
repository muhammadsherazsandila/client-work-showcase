## 🛍️ Zarandam — Premium Fashion E-Commerce

> **Client:** Zarandam Fashion Brand, Lahore, Pakistan  
> **Live:** [zarandam.com](https://zarandam.com)  
> **Status:** ✅ Live & Active  

📖 **[Read Full Case Study →](./case-studies/zarandam.md)**

### The Challenge
Zarandam, a premium Pakistani women's fashion brand, needed a modern e-commerce platform to showcase their embroidered collections and western wear to customers nationwide.

### What I Built
- Full-featured e-commerce platform with product catalog, cart management, and checkout
- Multi-category product browsing (embroidered suits, western wear, formal dresses)
- Integrated payment processing with Cash on Delivery (COD) and mobile wallet support
- Nationwide delivery system with free shipping on orders over PKR 6,000
- Returns/exchange management system (7-day policy)
- WhatsApp integration for customer support
- Fully responsive design optimized for mobile shoppers

### Tech Stack
`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `MongoDB` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `Cloudinary`

## 🚗 Khushi Riders — Multi-Service Platform

> **Client:** Khushi Riders, Rawalpindi/Islamabad, Pakistan  
> **Live:** [khushiriders.com](https://khushiriders.com)  
> **Status:** ✅ Live & Active  

📖 **[Read Full Case Study →](./case-studies/khushi-riders.md)**

### The Challenge
Khushi Riders, with 15+ years of experience in community services, needed a comprehensive web platform to unify their diverse service offerings — from transportation to education — under one digital roof.

### What I Built
- Multi-service platform with 6 distinct service verticals
- **Pick & Drop:** Booking system with verified drivers, monthly packages (10km/15km/20km tiers)
- **Car Rentals:** Premium vehicle rental listing and booking
- **Driving School:** Course enrollment and scheduling
- **Property Services:** Residential and commercial property listings (sales, purchases, rentals)
- **Car Sale & Purchase:** Vehicle marketplace
- **Home Tutoring:** Tutor matching for O/A Levels and Board examinations
- WhatsApp API integration for 24/7 customer support
- Driver verification and assignment workflow
- Privacy-compliant cookie and data management

### Tech Stack
`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB`
